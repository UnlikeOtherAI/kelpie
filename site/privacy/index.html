Kelpie Browser

Privacy Policy

Kelpie Browser is published by UnlikeOtherAI s.r.o. This policy explains what happens to your data when you browse, pair a device, or opt in to account and AI features.

Browsing and local storage

You can browse without an UnlikeOtherAI account. Kelpie stores browsing data such as open tabs, history, cookies, website storage, local bookmarks and browser settings on your device. Websites and search providers receive requests you make to them, along with any information you enter or submit. They handle that data under their own policies. Use Kelpie’s browser controls to manage history and website data on the device.

Paired devices and agent access

Kelpie can advertise its presence and basic device information, such as its device name and platform, on your local network. If you approve a computer, it can access the browser functions granted by that approval. Those functions can return page content, screenshots and other browsing data to the connected computer. An agent or tool running there may process what it receives. Review or revoke approvals in Kelpie’s paired-client settings.

When you sign in, Kelpie can connect to Nessie to make the browser available to your account. It sends an app-installation identifier, your device label, its platform and the browser features available for use. When you use Nessie for a browser task, it can receive the selected tab’s address, page content and any screenshots the task requests. You can pause or stop the connection in Kelpie.

Optional UnlikeOtherAI account

UnlikeOtherAI handles sign-in and account information under its account privacy policy. Kelpie uses that account for features such as synced favourites. If you save favourites to your account, UnlikeOtherAI stores the associated titles and URLs as settings for that account. Compatible signed-in Kelpie devices can also synchronise website cookies directly with one another on the same network over an encrypted connection. That cookie sync does not store cookies on the UnlikeOtherAI server. Signing out stops account sync on the device.

AI features

When you select an on-device model, it processes the supplied text on the device. If you configure and use an external AI endpoint, Kelpie sends the request, including any page content you supply, to that endpoint. The endpoint’s operator handles those requests under its own terms and privacy policy.

Retention, deletion and contact

Data stored locally remains on the device until you clear it, remove the relevant item, or remove the app and its data. To delete your UnlikeOtherAI account and its associated Kelpie account data, choose Delete account in Kelpie’s signed-in account menu or request account deletion online. Verify your account email and, if enabled, your authenticator code. UnlikeOtherAI reviews the request and contacts you if it needs anything else. Its account privacy policy explains retention and when it may need to keep records. Deleting local browser data does not remove copies already held by websites, connected clients or AI providers.

To ask about this policy or make a privacy request, email hello@unlikeotherai.com. Please do not send us passwords or verification codes.
